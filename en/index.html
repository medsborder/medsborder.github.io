Travel medication checker

Enter the active ingredients you take and the countries on your route: departure, layovers and destinations. You get a status per country, conclusions and links to check the rules yourself. Everything runs in your browser; what you type is not sent to us.

Check the result independently. This is not legal or medical advice. Rules change, and the data may be incomplete or out of date. “No data” never means “allowed”.

How it works

  1. Step 1: add the active ingredients you take. Type a name or add your own.
  2. Step 2: choose the departure country, any layovers and your destinations (up to ten countries in total).
  3. Step 3: press Check. You get a one-line verdict first, then a short list of what to do (do not bring, prepare, check yourself), your ingredients along the route, details by country with sources, and other countries with friendlier rules.

FAQ

Can I take my medicines to another country?

It depends on the country and on the ingredient. Medicines that contain controlled substances, such as some painkillers, sleeping pills and ADHD medicines, often need a prescription, a doctor’s certificate or a permit, and some countries ban substances that are common elsewhere. Check every country on your route and carry the original packaging and prescription.

Do layovers matter?

Yes. Rules for transit can differ from rules for entry, and some countries apply their controls even when you only change planes. Add your layovers to the route to see them in the results.

What does “no data” mean?

It means we have no verified source for that ingredient in that country. It never means the ingredient is allowed. Use the links to official sources or ask the embassy or customs of that country.

Can I check an ingredient that is not in the list?

Yes. Type its name and add it as a custom ingredient. We have no data for it, so you get links to search for it yourself.

Are the ingredients and countries I enter sent anywhere?

No. Everything is calculated in your browser and we have no server that receives your input. Only if you click a Google or AI assistant link (ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Gemini) are the names sent to those services. See the privacy policy for details.

Data comes from official lists with a “verified” date. Found a mistake? Please open an issue in the project repository.
