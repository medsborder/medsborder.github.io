Travel medication checker

Enter the active ingredients you take and the countries on your route: departure, layovers and destinations. You get a status per country, conclusions and links to check the rules yourself. Everything runs in your browser; what you type is not sent to us.

Check the result independently. This is not legal or medical advice. Rules change, and the data may be incomplete or out of date. “No data” never means “allowed”.

How it works

  1. Add at least one required active ingredient. Type a name or add your own.
  2. Choose the departure country, any layovers and up to nine more countries in total.
  3. Press Check. You see warnings when rules differ along the route, then conclusions and a table by country with sources.

Data comes from official lists with a “verified” date. Found a mistake? Please open an issue in the project repository.
