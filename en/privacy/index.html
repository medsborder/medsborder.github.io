Privacy and cookie policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026.

Who runs this site

MedsBorder is a free, static website run by the maintainers of the MedsBorder project (GitHub organisation “medsborder”). The maintainers decide how data is used and are the data controller for analytics. To reach us, open an issue in the project repository titled “Privacy request”. Do not put personal details in a public issue; we will reply with a private way to continue.

What you type stays with you

The ingredients and countries you enter are processed only in your browser. There is no server of ours, no account and no database, so we cannot see or store them. They are not sent to analytics. If you share a link from the results page, your choices are in the part of the address after the # sign. Browsers do not send that part to servers, but anyone you give the link to can read it.

What is stored on your device without asking

Two small items are stored locally in your browser (not sent anywhere): theme (your light, dark or system choice) and consent (your analytics choice and its date, kept for 180 days). They are needed for the site to work as you asked, so they do not need consent.

Analytics (only if you accept)

If you press “Accept analytics”, the site loads Google Tag Manager (container GTM-WJMLCVGW) and the tags configured in it, for example Google Analytics. Google sets cookies such as _ga and _ga_* and receives your IP address, device and browser details and the pages you view, so that we can count visits. We send only these events, and no ingredient or country names: check submitted (with how many ingredients and countries), link copied and external link clicked (only the type: Google, ChatGPT or a source). Until you accept, nothing from Google is loaded and no analytics cookie is set.

Legal basis and your choice

The basis is your consent (Article 6(1)(a) of the GDPR and the UK GDPR, and Article 5(3) of the ePrivacy Directive and the UK PECR). Accepting and rejecting are equally easy. You can change or withdraw consent at any time with “Cookie settings” in the footer; withdrawing turns analytics off and deletes the Google cookies. The site works the same either way. We ask again after 180 days.

Who receives the data

Google Ireland Limited and Google LLC process analytics data for us under Google’s data processing terms, which can involve transfers outside the EEA and the UK under standard contractual clauses or equivalent safeguards. How long Google Analytics keeps data is set in our analytics account; we aim for 14 months or less.

Links to Google and ChatGPT

The results page has links that open a Google search or ChatGPT with the ingredient and country names already filled in. Opening one sends those names to Google or OpenAI, which have their own privacy policies. We do not see what you do there. Links to official sources open the source’s own website.

Your rights

You can ask us for access to, correction or deletion of data we hold about you, restriction or objection to processing, portability, and you can withdraw consent at any time. Because we hold almost no personal data, most requests will be answered with “we do not hold any”. You may also complain to your data protection authority (for example the ICO in the UK, or your national authority in the EU).

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above changes. For material changes to analytics we will ask for consent again.
